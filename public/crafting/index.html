Dimraeth Crafting: Stations, Recipes & Salvaging Explained

Community records document 48 recipes across four crafting stations — this page explains the system so those recipes actually make sense: where you craft, where materials come from, and why the forge eats your old gear.

Documented recipes: 48Stations: 4Key habit: Salvage everything

Quick answer

Dimraeth crafting is a loop: gather materials in the world → salvage old gear at the forge → craft at stations → build up your stronghold. Community records document 48 recipes across four stations. The beginner-critical moves: don't vendor old gear (salvage it), stockpile Pinewood Planks early (the first real material gate), and cook — food buffs are the easiest power you're ignoring.

How the crafting loop works

  • Gather: wood, stone, ore, plants and drops come from specific regions — see the map guide for where each region sits.
  • Salvage: the forge breaks outdated weapons and armor down into crafting materials. Every level-up replaces gear, so salvaging is a steady material income — hoarding old gear is throwing away resources.
  • Craft at stations: each of the four stations covers a different family of recipes (smithing-style gear work, cooking, and processing among them). Recipes unlock as you progress and explore rather than being handed out at the start.
  • Build: crafted materials feed your stronghold — the base-building layer that unlocks after your first dungeon (see the beginner guide for that unlock sequence).

Crafting in co-op

Crafting materials and station access live in the shared world, so a little house-keeping goes a long way: agree on who hoards what, label chests, and remember a pet can ferry gathered loot back to base while everyone keeps adventuring. Groups that set up "dump chests" next to each station craft noticeably faster than groups with eight private stashes.

Honesty box: the 48-recipe / 4-station structure comes from community-maintained records checked in September 2026; per-recipe ingredient lists are still being verified by the community and change with EA patches. We deliberately publish the system-level guide first — the part that doesn't go stale — and will add recipe tables once the data settles.

FAQ

How does crafting work in Dimraeth?
Gather materials in the world, salvage old gear at the forge, then craft at four stations. Community records document 48 recipes in Early Access. Crafted materials also feed the stronghold base-building system.
Should I sell or salvage old gear in Dimraeth?
Salvage it at the forge. Salvaging converts outdated gear into crafting materials you'll need for your next tier — gold is easier to come by than materials.
What is the first crafting bottleneck in Dimraeth?
Pinewood Planks. Players consistently report them as the first material gate for early stronghold upgrades — they're processed in the Farmlands region, so stock up early.
How many crafting recipes are in Dimraeth?
Community records document 48 recipes across four crafting stations in Early Access, with more expected as EA content patches land.
