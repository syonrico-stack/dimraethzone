Dimraeth Max Level: The Early Access Cap & How to Level Fast

Wondering where the level bar stops? Early Access caps progression at level 25 — and the cap is designed to rise with each new act.

EA level cap: 25Cap raises: with each actAct 2: targeted 2027

Quick answer

The maximum level in Dimraeth Early Access is 25. Mudtek has said the cap will rise as new acts release (Act 2 is targeted for 2027). Hitting 25 is not the end of a character — gear sets, spellbooks, pets and base building continue to scale your power.

How leveling works in Dimraeth

Dimraeth uses eight attributes — Strength, Physical, Energy, Agility, Intelligence, Memory, Charisma and Adventure — and XP costs differ per archetype. Your archetype's "primary" attribute gives the most value per point, so leveling efficiently means feeding your primary first and dipping into secondary stats only for specific spellbook or gear requirements.

  • Primary attribute first: e.g. Horned Vanguard starts with 10 Strength — keep pushing it
  • Spellbook gates: many spellbooks have attribute requirements; check before spending
  • Adventure stat: contributes utility bonuses (e.g. Magic Damage for Elemental Ranger) — easy to underrate
  • Respec exists: skill respec lets you experiment, but stat resets need more planning — don't scatter points early

The fastest route to 25

  1. Follow the main questline through Earlwood Village and the Farmlands — quests are the densest XP source in act 1
  2. Clear boss bounties — they are part of the gear progression loop and pay out reliable XP
  3. Farm the Corrupted Forest once your build comes online; elite packs there out-value earlier zones
  4. Play co-op — 1–8 player groups clear content faster, and your character keeps the XP wherever you play
  5. Don't hoard consumables — food and potion buffs accelerate every fight; the Sanctum's farms and crafting stations exist to keep you supplied

What to do at the cap

Level 25 is a plateau, not a wall. The endgame loop for now: complete gear sets (mixing set bonuses beats auto-completing one set), hunt the nine pets for Nine Lives, recruit every Sanctum ally, finish achievements, and prepare for the Q4 2026 patch — Goblin Caves, a world boss, a new dungeon and difficulty tiers will give capped characters fresh reasons to grind. See the roadmap page for dates.

FAQ

What is the max level in Dimraeth?
Level 25 in Early Access. Mudtek has said the cap rises with each new act — Act 2 is targeted for 2027.
Can you respec in Dimraeth?
Yes — a skill respec system exists for experimenting with builds, though full stat resets require more planning. Race and class themselves cannot be changed after creation.
Is there anything to do after max level?
Yes: gear set hunting, all 9 pets, Sanctum allies, achievements, and preparing for the Q4 2026 content patch (Goblin Caves, world boss, new dungeon, difficulty tiers).
