Dimraeth Mods: What's Available & How to Install Them

Two weeks into Early Access, Dimraeth already has a small but real mod scene — and no official mod support yet. Here's what exists, where to get it, and how not to break your save.

Nexus Mods: 16 modsSteam Workshop: Not yetTrainers: WeMod + others

Quick answer

Yes, Dimraeth has mods. As of late September 2026, Nexus Mods lists 16 Dimraeth mods — mostly quality-of-life and balance tweaks — installed manually or via community mod loaders (the open-source "Grovekeeper" toolkit uses MelonLoader). There is no Steam Workshop support yet. Always back up your save before modding, and expect patches to break mods until authors update them.

How to install (the safe routine)

  1. Back up your save first. Dimraeth characters are precious — copy your save folder somewhere safe before touching anything.
  2. Read the mod page fully. Check it was updated after the latest EA patch; an outdated mod is the #1 cause of broken saves.
  3. Follow the loader's instructions (Grovekeeper/MelonLoader for code mods; simple file-copy for asset tweaks).
  4. Add one mod at a time and launch the game between installs — when something breaks you'll know exactly which mod did it.
  5. After every game patch, assume mods are broken until their pages say otherwise.

Mods and co-op: the honest warning

Dimraeth's co-op syncs a shared world across up to 8 players. Mods that change items, stats or world logic can desync or corrupt that shared world if players run different mod sets. House rules that work: everyone installs the identical mod list, the host backs up the world before sessions, and trainers/cheats stay in single-player. There are no reported bans — Dimraeth has no anti-cheat — but a corrupted 40-hour world needs no ban hammer to ruin your week.

Honesty box: mod counts and tooling change weekly during Early Access. The numbers above were checked in late September 2026; click through to Nexus Mods for the live list. We link out rather than mirror files, so you always get the author's current version.

FAQ

Are there mods for Dimraeth?
Yes. Nexus Mods listed 16 Dimraeth mods two weeks into Early Access, mostly quality-of-life and balance tweaks, plus community mod-loading tools like the open-source Grovekeeper toolkit.
Does Dimraeth have Steam Workshop support?
Not yet. As of September 2026 all modding is manual or via community loaders. No Workshop support has been announced for the EA period.
Are Dimraeth mods safe? Can you get banned?
Dimraeth has no anti-cheat system, so bans aren't the risk — broken saves are. Back up your save, install one mod at a time, and keep your mod list identical across co-op players.
Is there a Dimraeth trainer?
Yes — WeMod released a trainer with 17 cheats (health, stamina, resources and similar). Trainers are fine for single-player learning; keep them out of shared co-op worlds.
