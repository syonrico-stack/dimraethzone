Dimraeth Best Class: All 9 Archetypes Ranked for Your First Run

In Dimraeth you don't pick a class — you pick a race × class combination called an archetype. Here is the full launch table, what each one plays like, and which to start with.

Archetypes at EA launch: 9Beginner pick: Horned VanguardFlexible pick: Elemental Ranger

Quick answer

For the easiest first run, take Minotaur Brawler (Horned Vanguard) — it starts with 10 Strength, 8 Physical, 8 Energy and a wide-hitting battle axe, so ordinary early fights are comfortable before any gear or spellbooks. Want ranged flexibility? Human Magician (Elemental Ranger). Want speed? Elf Shadow (Night's Whisper).

So… what is the best class?

There is no single best class — each archetype has a "primary" attribute that gives the most value per point, nudging you toward a build without forcing it. The real question is which combat rhythm you enjoy:

  • Easiest first run: Horned Vanguard — wide melee coverage deletes early packs before you own any specialized gear
  • Most flexible: Elemental Ranger — crossbow when melee is scary, sword and spells when it isn't
  • Highest tempo: Night's Whisper — constant repositioning, crits and plague-style status
  • Best caster: Echo Weaver — pure spellcasting with time magic utility
  • Most unusual: Earthshaker — a Strength-scaling battle-mage; great in co-op where allies can hold aggro

Whichever you pick, commit for a few hours before judging — early quests hand out enough XP, skills and gear to reveal whether the rhythm fits. Builds for each archetype live in our build guide, and attribute XP costs in the leveling guide.

FAQ

What is the best class in Dimraeth?
No single best — but Minotaur Brawler (Horned Vanguard) is the most forgiving start: 10 Strength at level 1 and a wide-swinging battle axe. Elemental Ranger (Human Magician) is the most flexible.
How many archetypes are in Dimraeth?
Nine in Early Access (3 races × 3 classes). The 1.0 plan is 48 archetypes from 8 races and 6 classes.
What is an archetype in Dimraeth?
Your race + class combination. It has its own name, skill tree, passive, weapon setup and primary attribute — race is a mechanical choice, not a cosmetic one.
