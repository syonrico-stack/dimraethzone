Dimraeth Spells: All 46 Documented Spells by School

Mudtek advertises 300+ spells for the full game. In Early Access, the community has so far documented 46 named spells across 11 schools — here is every one of them, plus how to think about your 5-slot loadout.

Documented spells: 46Schools: 11Loadout slots: 5Full-game target: 300+

Quick answer

You don't pick "a spell" in Dimraeth — you build a 5-slot loadout from every spell you've learned. New spells come from spellbooks found as loot and rewards. The 46 spells documented so far spread across 11 schools: Fire (9), Earth (7), Water, Nature and Holy (5 each), Physical and the universal core kit (4 each), Air (3), Cold (2), Poison and Blood (1 each). A good loadout = 1 sustain + 1 mobility + 2 damage from different schools + 1 control.

What about "Dark"?

Community records file four more entries under "Dark": Basic Attack, Sprint, Sneak and Throw Stone. In practice these behave like the universal starter kit every character has rather than a school you invest in — that brings the documented total to 46.

How to learn new spells

Spells are learned from spellbooks — found as loot, quest rewards and vendor stock. Early priorities: clear the tutorial cave thoroughly, check the Earlwood vendors after each quest turn-in, and loot every bookshelf-style interactable in dungeons. Because your loadout is limited to five slots, a new spell is never a burden — it's another option to swap in.

Building your 5-slot loadout

  • Slot 1 — Sustain: Heal (Nature) or Ring of Healing (Holy). Solo players feel the difference immediately.
  • Slot 2 — Mobility: Leap (Physical) gets you out of boss slams; Sprint covers ground between waypoints.
  • Slots 3–4 — Damage from two different schools: resistances vary by enemy family; a Fire + Earth pairing covers far more situations than doubling one school.
  • Slot 5 — Control: Gravity Well, Black Hole or Frost Pillar turn messy pulls into target practice.

Match your loadout to your archetype's attributes — a Minotaur Brawler gets more from Physical and Earth than from a mana-hungry Fire stack. For attribute synergy and gear direction, see the build guide.

FAQ

How many spells are in Dimraeth?
Mudtek advertises 300+ spells for the full game. In Early Access the community has documented 46 named spells across 11 schools so far, plus the universal starter kit (Basic Attack, Sprint, Sneak, Throw Stone).
How do you learn new spells in Dimraeth?
From spellbooks — found as loot, quest rewards and in vendor stock. Learned spells stay available permanently and you swap them into your 5-slot loadout.
How many spells can you equip in Dimraeth?
Five at a time. A common framework is 1 sustain, 1 mobility, 2 damage spells from different schools, and 1 control spell.
What are the best spells in Dimraeth?
For most builds: Heal or Ring of Healing for sustain, Leap for mobility, and one control spell like Gravity Well or Frost Pillar. Fill the last two slots with damage from two different schools to cover enemy resistances.
