About DimraethZone

A fast, honest, player-written guide hub for Dimraeth Early Access.

What this site is

DimraethZone is an unofficial fan site covering Mudtek's pixel action RPG Dimraeth — classes, races, archetypes, pets, maps, builds and co-op. We launched in September 2026 alongside the game's Early Access release.

Our standard

Every guide is cross-checked against official sources (Steam page, Mudtek announcements) and multiple player reports. Where information is community-sourced or unverified, we label it as such instead of presenting guesses as fact. Early Access games change fast — pages carry an implicit "as of the current build" stamp and are updated as patches land.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Mudtek. Dimraeth and all related names, art and marks belong to their respective owners. If anything here is inaccurate, it will be corrected promptly.
