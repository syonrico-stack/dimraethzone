Dimraeth Map: Every Region of Mirkea in Early Access

From your home Sanctum to the Corrupted Forest — the named regions of the Early Access world, what lives in each, and what's coming with the Q4 patch.

World: Mirkea (handcrafted)Coming Q4 2026: Goblin Caves

Quick answer

Early Access opens with the regions around Earlwood: The Sanctum (your base), Earlwood Village, the Farmlands, South Earlwood, and North Earlwood with the Corrupted Forest. Further out lie the Wildwood (Alpha Wolf), The Shard Court, the Golem's Garden, Corin and the Beast Occupied Ruins. The Goblin Caves arrive with the Q4 2026 patch.

What's not on the map yet

Mudtek's roadmap names the Goblin Caves (Q4 2026, with a world boss, a new dungeon and difficulty tiers) and Act 2 (2027) with new regions and a higher level cap. The in-game map is hand-drawn rather than procedural, so new regions arrive as crafted spaces, not generated filler.

Exploration tip: several pet unlocks and quest steps reference landmarks the in-game map labels only faintly (the "over-run shrine" north of Corin is the classic example). When a guide gives you a compass direction, trust it — the community's directions have proven accurate so far.

FAQ

How big is the Dimraeth map?
Early Access covers act 1 of Mirkea: the Earlwood regions, the Corrupted Forest, the Wildwood and outlying landmarks like The Shard Court and Corin. Act 2 (2027) adds new regions, and the Goblin Caves arrive in the Q4 2026 patch.
Where is the Frost Owl in Dimraeth?
Southwest of Corin, north of the over-run shrine, per player reports. Once unlocked, buy it from Myrll's shop.
Is the Dimraeth map procedurally generated?
No — the world is handcrafted pixel art. New regions arrive through content patches rather than procedural generation.
