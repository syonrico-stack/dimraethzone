Dimraeth Release Date, Price & Early Access Roadmap

Everything about Dimraeth's launch and what's coming next — kept current as patches land.

EA launch: Sep 15, 2026Price: $21.99EA window: ~18–24 months

Quick answer

Dimraeth launched into Steam Early Access on September 15, 2026 for $21.99 (the 10% launch discount ended September 22). It's Windows PC with Steam Deck support; consoles are unconfirmed. Next up: a Q4 2026 content patch, then Act 2 targeted for 2027.

Platforms & tech

  • PC (Windows) via Steam — the only platform at EA launch
  • Steam Deck supported for handheld play
  • Consoles (PS5/Xbox/Switch): not confirmed — any site claiming dates is speculating
  • Demo: the pre-launch demo is offline; progress does not carry over — EA is a fresh start

Is it worth buying in Early Access?

Launch reception has been strong — the game sits at 9/10 on Steam user reviews in its first week, with particular praise for the archetype system and the Sanctum base loop. The honest caveats: act 1 is all there is for now, the level cap is 25, and race/class/appearance choices are locked at creation. If you enjoy watching a game grow — and want your archetype knowledge to carry into 1.0 — EA is a good entry point. If you want the finished 48-archetype game, wishlist and wait.
