Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

DimraethZone ("we", "this site") is an unofficial fan-made guide website. This policy explains what data is collected when you visit.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic (pages visited, approximate region, device type). Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data under Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out with browser settings or Google's opt-out tools.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled on this site in the future (for example Google AdSense), third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Embedded content

Pages may embed YouTube videos (via youtube-nocookie.com) which may still collect usage data per Google's policies when played.

What we don't collect

We do not run user accounts, comments, newsletters or contact forms, and we do not sell any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the contact information on our About page.
