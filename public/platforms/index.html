Dimraeth Platforms: PS5, Xbox, Switch & Steam Deck Status

One page to answer every "is Dimraeth on…" question — what's confirmed, what's not, and what Mudtek has actually said about consoles and handhelds.

Available now: PC (Steam)Price: $21.99Consoles: Not announced

Quick answer

Dimraeth is currently PC-only, in Steam Early Access at $21.99 (launched September 15, 2026). There is no announced PS5, Xbox or Switch version — Mudtek is a tiny indie team focused on the PC roadmap through the 18–24 month Early Access period. No console or Game Pass release should be expected before 1.0 at the earliest.

Honesty box: console timing is the most-misreported Dimraeth topic on the internet — several auto-generated wiki pages list "Switch/PS5 versions" with invented dates. As of late September 2026 there is no official console announcement of any kind. When that changes, this page changes the same day.

What about the demo?

The free Dimraeth demo (a limited-slice playtest) closed on July 15, 2026, ahead of the Early Access launch. It is no longer downloadable — anyone searching "Dimraeth demo" today should go straight to the EA build; everything in the demo exists in expanded form there.

Why consoles will probably wait for 1.0

Mudtek's published roadmap is explicit about sequencing: Q4 2026 content patch, two race/class updates, then Act 2 in 2027, with 1.0 targeted after an 18–24 month EA run. Console ports for a 3-person indie team almost always follow the finished PC build — see the release date & roadmap page for the full timeline.

FAQ

Is Dimraeth on PS5?
No. Dimraeth is PC-only (Steam Early Access) and no PlayStation version has been announced. Don't expect console news before the 1.0 release at the earliest.
Is Dimraeth on Xbox or Game Pass?
No — there is no Xbox version and no Game Pass announcement as of September 2026.
Is Dimraeth on Nintendo Switch?
No Switch or Switch 2 version has been announced. Dimraeth is PC-only during Early Access.
Can you play Dimraeth on Steam Deck?
There is no official Steam Deck verified rating yet, but the developer has signaled that handheld play matters to them and community reports say it runs. Check the Steam store page for the official compatibility badge.
