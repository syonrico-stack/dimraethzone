What is Dimraeth?

Long ago in the Lands of Mirkea, the Human Kingdom of Aldoria and the Minotaur Empire of Xwin fought an endless war. Two deserters — Tolais of Aldoria and Antark of Xwin — founded the sanctuary city of Dimraeth in magic-saturated mountains. When the self-proclaimed Dark Lord Sylbos bargained for immortality, the wizard Faustus shattered the ritual — and the broken magic scattered immortality across a handful of unsuspecting souls. You play one of them.

Mechanically, Dimraeth blends deliberate stamina-based combat with a character builder: your race and class fuse into one of nine launch archetypes, each with its own skill tree and passive. Between expeditions you return to the Sanctum — your upgradeable stronghold with farms, crafting stations, recruit-able allies and pets.

FAQ

Is Dimraeth out now?
Yes. Dimraeth launched in Steam Early Access on September 15, 2026 for $21.99, developed and published by Mudtek. The full 1.0 release is expected after roughly 18–24 months of Early Access.
Is Dimraeth on PS5, Xbox or Switch?
Not at Early Access launch — it is PC (Windows) only, with Steam Deck support. Consoles have not been confirmed. The store's '8 races and 6 classes' describes the 1.0 goal; Early Access currently has 3 races and 3 classes.
How many players is Dimraeth co-op?
Solo or online co-op for up to 8 players with drop-in/drop-out. Character progress is tied to you, while world progress is tied to the host's save.
What is the best class in Dimraeth for beginners?
There is no single best class, but the most forgiving start is Minotaur Brawler (Horned Vanguard) thanks to 10 starting Strength and wide melee coverage. See our best class guide for all 9 archetypes.
